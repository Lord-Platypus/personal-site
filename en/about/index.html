About

I'm Emanuele Vinci, a freelance app developer. I was born in Milan and for a few years now I've lived near Verona, where I work and teach.

I've been building native and web apps with Flutter since 2020, freelance, for private companies. Over the years I've worked across very different fields: a banking app, where I recently served as frontend team lead handling architecture, code review and team coordination; a delivery app for a pastry shop, with payments, notifications and backend communication; a parking-reservation app with in-app purchases and a built-in Google Maps navigator; a management system for an orthopaedic medical practice, with document handling and a clinical-path editor; and an app for post-operative eye tests, managing documents, images and video.

These projects are covered by non-disclosure agreements. An interactive demo, for illustration only, is available here: lord-platypus.github.io/portfolio — real projects are under NDA.

Beyond development I focus on teaching, and I co-founded Game Dev Arena, through which I take part in the local game-development community and its events. I enjoy building products as much as teaching others to build them.
